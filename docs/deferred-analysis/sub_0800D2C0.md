## NEW this round (KEEP — cleaner than round-16's reasoning)
1. **flags split = the counter↔flags register swap fix (rederived, no high-reg pin on flags).**
   Round 16 used `u16 flags1; u16 flags2;` in its var-list but the import here started from a
   single `u16 flags`. With ONE `flags`, the loop COUNTER and FLAGS compete for {r6,r7} and
   agbcc swaps them (counter→r7, flags→r6; target wants counter→r6, flags→r7). VERIFIED via
   instrumented global.c `find_reg`: with one shared `flags`, the flags pseudo (nrefs 12,
   livelen 76, priority floor_log2(12)*12/76 = 0.474) OUTRANKS the counter pseudo (nrefs 8,
   livelen 64, priority 0.375) → flags allocated first → grabs r6 (lowest free). SPLITTING
   flags into per-loop `flags1`/`flags2` drops each to nrefs 6 / livelen ~38 → BELOW the
   counter → counter wins r6, flags1/flags2 take r7. This is the documented escape and it is
   PURE C (no pin). It took byte_diff 255→251 and removed the whole counter/flags cascade.
   The two-stage prologue (ux→r9, uy→r8, mode→sl, order `ux; sx; uy; sy`) and the
   scol(r3)/srow(r4) materialized-compare pins from round 16 are still needed and KEPT.

## Remaining wall (byte_diff 251 / diff_count 86): the regmove COPY-SPREAD — PROVEN unreachable
The baserom (target 400 B) has ~15 MORE instructions than our build (376 B). They are all
the regmove `copy_src_to_dest` copies that hold scol(r3)/srow(r4)/dcol in their home reg and
COPY them to caller-saved scratch (r0/r1/r2) before each use, e.g.
`adds r2,r3,#0; subs r3,r2,#3` (scol copy) and `adds r1,r4,#0; cmp r1,#4` … `adds r0,r1,#0;
cmp r0,#4` (srow spread r4→r1→r0). Our agbcc emits the in-place 2-address form
(`subs r3,#3`, `cmp r4,#4`) — FEWER, shorter insns.

### Why it does NOT fire for our C — PROVEN by instrumenting regmove (THE new evidence)
Built a private instrumented old_agbcc (copy of the symlinked tools/agbcc-src to /tmp,
fprintf at `gcc/regmove.c` line ~2780 `if (! (src_note = find_reg_note(insn, REG_DEAD, src)))`
+ at the `copy_src_to_dest` call site, rebuilt to a private prefix, compiled ONLY this TU,
then `rm -rf`'d — the shared toolchain was never touched). Result: **the regmove probe
printed NOTHING for our C.** `copy_src_to_dest` requires SRC to NOT be dead at the
two-address insn (no REG_DEAD note). In our C, scol DIES at `dcol = scol - 3` → clean
in-place `subs r3,#3` → no copy. The baserom's scol does NOT die there → copy. To make scol
not-die you must USE it after the subtract, but every such shape either (a) CSE-folds the
recompute back to dcol AND re-deads scol (still in-place), or (b) survives as a REAL second
`subs` (size grows to 388, byte_diff 266). Catch-22 with this agbcc config. The srow spread
is the same mechanism (srow in r4 must be copied to scratch only if read through a fresh
non-coalescing pseudo — every split `rowA=srow; rowB=rowA;` and every `(s32)(u32)` cast
COALESCED away, unlike D270's `row=(s32)y` which survived ONLY because `y` is a PARAMETER
pseudo, not a local).
