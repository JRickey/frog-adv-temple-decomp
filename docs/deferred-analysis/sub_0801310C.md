     asrs r0,r2;ands r0,r3`.
   - built spreads i across r3 and RE-materializes the `1` constant

     `ands r7,r0` (const in r7) instead of the target's `ands r7,r3`

The blocker is that the `on asm("r7")` pin (required so `on` survives the two
BLs as the srcIndex arg — unpinning it regresses to diff 209, on lands in the
wrong reg and breaks the call args) forces the extraction's operand order and
defeats the constant-1 CSE. Need a formulation that BOTH keeps `on` in r7
across the calls AND lets agbcc load the shifted s5 directly into r7 (`ldrh r7`
then `asr/and` in place) with the `1` CSE'd in r3.
