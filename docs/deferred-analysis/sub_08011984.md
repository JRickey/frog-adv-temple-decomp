## Baserom register map (ground truth, re-confirmed round 16)

- elems → r9 (kept head→tail)
- idx   → ip (kept head→tail; copied to a low reg via `mov r0,ip` when used in arith)
- idx8 (= idx<<3) → r8 at the head, SPILLED to [sp,#12] before the loop, RELOADED at tail
- 6480-base → r1 (head stores), then **`mov sl,r1`** relocates it to sl (tail field_34/36

  `mov r8,r1` carries it into the loop (r8 is free because idx8 was just spilled).
- 6540-base → reloaded each use (r4, r7), never kept.
- frame = 16 (3 outgoing stack args for the 7-arg call + idx8 spill).

### NEW LEVER #2 — defeat the field_18 DSE with a literal-address cast

## THE REMAINING WALL (precisely characterized)

The baserom defeats the DSE WITHOUT a pool literal via a **live-range split**:
6480-base r1→sl. That split fires only when the **60A0/dst-base is forced into
r1** (reusing the 6480-base's reg). And 60A0/dst-base is forced into r1 only when
**r8 is already occupied by idx8** (so 60A0 can't grab a free callee-saved reg and
must relocate into r1, evicting 6480 to sl). So the whole match hinges on ONE
emergent coloring state:

    idx → ip   AND   idx8 → r8 (spilled across the loop)   AND   60A0/dst → r1

Lever #1 reliably gets idx→ip + elems→r9. The unsolved piece is getting **idx8
into r8 AND spilled across the loop** (baserom: idx8 in r8 through the head,
spilled to [sp,#12] before the loop, reloaded at tail). Every clean shape tried
puts idx8 in **sl** (then 60A0 takes r8, no relocation, DSE fires) OR keeps idx8
in r8 UNSPILLED (frame 12, tail recomputes — also wrong). idx8 needs allocno
priority above elems' 0.0800 to be ordered before elems and claim r8; idx8 is
refs=3 len=62 → pri 0.0484, allocated late → sl/spill. Shortening idx8's range to
≤37 (→pri>0.079) is blocked because idx8 is genuinely used at head AND tail.
